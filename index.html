    Chapter Eight

    A Letter I Want You To Keep Forever

    Dear Kishu,

    I've started this letter more times than I can count, because no version of it has ever felt big enough for what I'm trying to say. So I'll tell you the truth, the way I would at 2am, when the world is quiet and it's just us.

    Before you, I had a life. It was fine. It was mine. I just didn't know it was missing something until you walked into it, and every ordinary day quietly started becoming extraordinary.

    I think about the small things most. The way you laugh at your own jokes before you've even finished telling them. The way you steal the last bite off my plate without asking, and somehow it still feels sweet. The way you fall asleep mid‑sentence and swear you were listening the whole time. I love our small things more than I know how to say.

    I think about we meet first time more often than you probably realize, and about how it's still one of my favorite days of my life. I think about the nights we talked until neither of us could keep our eyes open, because we'd rather be tired together than well-rested apart.

    You didn't just make my life better — you made it make sense. You gave ordinary Tuesdays a reason to smile. You made me want to be steadier, kinder, softer — not because you asked me to, but because loving you well is something I actually want to practice, every single day.

    I don't want a future that doesn't have you standing in the middle of it. I want the version of us where we're mildly annoyed at each other over something small on a Wednesday, and completely certain about each other anyway. I want slow mornings, and a home that smells like whatever we're cooking badly together, and a life that looks a little messy and a lot like us.

    I want to grow old with you. I want to still be choosing you when we're both wrinkled and slow, telling the same old stories to people who've heard them a hundred times. I want us — in every version of the future I let myself imagine.

    I don't know exactly what the future will look like...

    but I know who I want beside me when I find out.

    You. ❤️

    Forever yours.
